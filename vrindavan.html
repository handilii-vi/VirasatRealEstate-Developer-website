Overview

Not just a colony, a spiritually enriched ecosystem

Drushika Vrindavan is not just a housing development. Designed to reflect the timeless beauty and harmony of Lord Krishna's Vrindavan, it blends traditional architecture with modern amenities.

Every element, whether architecture, landscaping or lifestyle space, reflects the stories and serenity of Krishna Leela. Seven themed vatikas, each a chapter from Krishna's life, encourage spiritual growth, wellness and a deeper connection with nature.

Seven chapters

The seven vatikas

Chapter 01

Madhuban Vatika

Garden of Playfulness

Celebrates the joyful childhood days of Lord Krishna.

Chapter 02

Govardhan Vatika

Garden of Friendship

Recalls the unity of the day Krishna lifted Govardhan and everyone stood together.

Chapter 03

Vishram Vatika

Garden of Relaxation

A peaceful space created for rest and calm.

Chapter 04

Raas Vatika

Garden of Love

Reflects the divine Raas Leela of Lord Krishna.

Chapter 05

Dyut Vatika

Garden of Warfare and Wisdom

Symbol of the Mahabharat game of dice, reflecting Krishna's divine wisdom and strategy.

Chapter 06

Krishna Vatika

Garden of Worship

Dedicated to devotion and worship, with sacred trees and seating for pooja.

Chapter 07

Leela Vatika

Garden of Magic

A glimpse of Krishna's divine leelas, with a kitchen garden and statues depicting his pastimes.
