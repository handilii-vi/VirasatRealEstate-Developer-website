Overview

A grand address, spread over 100+ acres

Drushika Imperial & Paradise Grande is a grand plotted community in Indore's Super Corridor, spread over more than 100 acres. With an open sitting area and temple, a yoga and meditation deck, gardens and multiple play zones, it is planned for families who want space, calm and connectivity together.

Plots are Vastu compliant, with complete light and water facilities, and loan facility is available from public and private banks.
